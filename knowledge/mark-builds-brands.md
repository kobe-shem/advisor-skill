## Core doctrine (distilled, evergreen)

- **Depth is the #1 predictor of ad profitability.** Most ads fail because they speak one layer deep.
  Speak to the customer at the deepest layer you can, across five increasing layers:
  (1) **conscious thoughts** — "enter the conversation in their mind" (Collier); good, but conscious
  thought is a sliver of cognition; (2) **emotions** — people buy on emotion and justify with logic
  (Ogilvy/Ziglar); the emotional reaction fires before the rational one, so the flow is *feel → think
  → act*; (3) **belief system** — emotions come from beliefs; the brain is a prediction machine that
  only updates on contradicting evidence ("prediction error"), so claims get filtered out until you
  shift the belief. **Marketing is belief-change work: install empowering beliefs, remove limiting
  ones.** (4) **identity** — people act in accordance with self-image; use **identity labeling**
  (repeatedly assign the buyer an identity aligned with the purchase, leaning on "you/you are" +
  repetition + emotion to reach the subconscious); (5) **consciousness / energy level** — meet the
  buyer where they're calibrated (often guilt/fear/grief/apathy), lift them, then bring them back down
  to maximize the **emotional delta** (the size of the felt change). Use *all* layers in one ad; don't
  write surface-level "ChatGPT-ass copy."

- **Ad structure = emotional hook → logical justification → CTA** (the same as Hormozi's hook → meat →
  CTA). The hook must fire the emotion hard; then stack credibility/proof/authority/studies as the
  *justification* after they're emotionally bought in. Comedy openers rarely scale on cold traffic
  (shallow emotion) — the exception proves the rule, don't be the exception.

- **The winning SALES MESSAGE is the asset — not "the winning ad."** Once you find the core
  message/angle that resonates, it transmutes into every format (video, UGC, VSL, static, native,
  founder UGC). A product has many buy-reasons; each reason is an angle. Find the message **fast and
  cheap with image ads** (cheap, fast, lets you test many angles/avatars/messaging before investing in
  video). Early ad spend = **buying data, not making money.**

- **AI's real edge is SPEED to acquire data**, not "saving time." The whole game is the feedback loop
  (try → data → revise); AI collapses it from weeks to hours, so you can find the winning message far
  faster. Idea → live business in under a day.

- **Four business variables, in priority order: Market > Product > Ads > Funnel.**
  - **Market** is the most powerful lever. Pick for **urgency** (how fast they want it solved),
    **high pain** (physical *or* emotional — e.g. pets), and **high TAM**. Mark explicitly rejects
    "riches in the niches" for scale — big brands play huge markets (weight loss, pets, GLP-1). Market
    choice should take <30 min. Classic filter: sell something that helps them make money, improve
    relationships/sex, or lose weight.
  - **Product is NOT king.** EQ-balance metaphor: product, ads, and funnel are levers you balance — a
    10/10 product can carry weak ads/funnel, but a 5/10 product means ads + funnel must carry it.
    Don't reflexively blame the product. Criteria: solves a problem in a passionate market;
    **≥ $30 gross margin AND ≥ 3× COGS**; bonus: fits in a shoebox (logistics).

- **Funnel: never send cold traffic straight to the PDP.** Run an **advertorial pre-lander** (ad +
  editorial) that sells subconsciously so the buyer doesn't feel sold to: ad → advertorial → sales
  page → order page. Cheap cold traffic is less qualified, so the funnel must do the convincing. Every
  **lander image must install a belief or overcome an objection** — never decorative (and ad images ≠
  lander images).

- **AI ad production (the anti-slop craft).** Everything stems from **foundational docs** = deep
  psychographic research (exact customer words, emotional hot points, sophistication + awareness
  stage). "The money's in the research," but AI makes it fast. Two ways to create winners: **swipe** a
  proven 4-5★ competitor ad (reduce variables) or generate **new concepts** (AI supplies the
  originality). Pipeline: swipe/concept → Claude analyzes *why it works* and reverse-engineers every
  element to your brand + outputs a **text-to-image prompt** → Higgsfield **Nano Banana Pro** (upload a
  product PNG on white bg, 9:16) → for video, **image-to-video with Kling** from that starting frame.
  - **Hook ≈ 90% of a video ad's success** (first 3-5s): copy (~70-80%) + scroll-stopper clip ("what
    did I just look at?") + audio (least important). Then the body sells (mechanism, differentiation)
    → hard CTA.
  - **Prompt depth is what kills AI slop.** Garbled text / wrong labels = insufficient prompt detail.
    Spell out every label line verbatim; specify lens/focal length/DOF/lighting; specify what is **NOT**
    in frame (the image is the frozen moment *before* the action). For image-to-video: anchor events to
    exact words, state what stays still (label legibility), specify delivery style precisely, and treat
    one clip as one continuous shot (cuts happen in the editor). Iterate aggressively.

- **Meta delivery is an equation, not luck** (Mark's "21-min algorithm" video — overlaps Nathan's
  card; Nathan is deeper). Four steps in ~200ms: **retrieval** (tens of millions → millions; *creative
  is the targeting* — Meta reads hook/format/demographic/copy/script/thumbnail/landing-page to decide
  who sees it; the **Andromeda** update re-sorted retrieval as AI creative flooded the platform, so
  creative drives targeting, not ad-set audiences) → **light ranking** → **heavy ranking** (where the
  value equation applies) → **auction**. **Total value ≈ (bid × estimated action rate) + a
  consumer-experience term**; for conversion ads, eAR ≈ estimated CTR × estimated click-to-conversion.
  So: maximize CTR + all soft metrics (CPC/CPM/hook rate/hold rate) *and* conversion rate, without
  trashing UX. Practical posture: run **broad / Advantage+**, give Meta control *within reason* (its
  in-app suggestions are often money-draining), and pick bidding (lowest-cost vs cost/bid cap) by the
  business constraint — both scale.
  - **Learning phase is overrated** — ads are always learning and everything eventually dies; at low
    spend, results look like a sine wave (normal). **"When in doubt, zoom out"** — judge on 7+ days /
    max data, not daily emotion.
  - **EMQ / CAPI:** send good server-side data, but Mark has run 9.8-EMQ / "0 opportunity score"
    accounts that were highly profitable — take the score (and *all* in-platform conversion data) with
    a grain of salt; trust third-party attribution.
  - **Creative diversity:** variations are still highly effective post-Andromeda — an ad set = one
    net-new concept, ads 2-4 = variations (more at-bats at the concept level); a variation hook often
    wins, so don't believe "only new concepts survive Andromeda."
  - **Think like an entrepreneur, not a media buyer**, and **question everything** — Meta is a
    for-profit public company; ban waves track its earnings reports, not just your creative.

## Decision rules
- If ads spend and return nothing → you're speaking too shallow. Name the **belief to shift** and the
  **identity to assign** before writing a word; hook on emotion, justify with proof.
- If copy reads like ChatGPT → rewrite to hit emotion first, anchor in belief + identity, then justify.
- If choosing what to build → fix the **market** first (urgency × pain × TAM), then the **product**
  (≥$30 GM and ≥3× COGS, shoebox), then pour effort into **ads + funnel** (the cheap-to-fix levers).
- If cold traffic converts poorly → it's the funnel: insert an **advertorial** before the sales page;
  make every lander image install a belief or kill an objection.
- If an AI image/video has garbled text or a wrong label → add prompt detail (spell the label verbatim,
  lens/DOF, what's NOT in frame) and iterate — don't abandon the concept.
- If you want to scale creative → find the winning **sales message** with cheap image ads first, then
  transmute it into video/UGC/VSL/native.
- If a "new concept" looks like a loser → test its **variations** (different hooks) before killing it.
- If Meta results swing daily → zoom out to 7+ days; don't make emotional cuts; don't blindly follow
  Meta's in-app suggestions.
