## Claims

**NP-AS-01.** Structure evolves by spend tier: under $50K/mo, one dedicated testing campaign (ABO, or Advantage+ with minimum spend caps per ad set) plus an Advantage+ scaling campaign with existing-customer exclusions; $50–250K/mo, multiple Advantage+ campaigns segmented by concept (product/collection for fashion); $250K+/mo, add a second ad account.
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 0:36–1:11, 2:49, 15:00

**NP-AS-02.** A testing ad set bundles 3–5 (up to 6) creatives around ONE concept; success is judged by a KPI at the ad-set level, never the ad level. Winners graduate to the scaling campaign; losers feed iterative test design (never repeat the failed creative-type × angle × persona combo).
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 1:13–1:58, 17:08–17:25

**NP-AS-03.** A concept = offer × angle × audience (≈ persona). One offer can spawn 20 angles × 20 audiences — thousands of concepts — but only 2–4 will run efficiently at scale; consolidate into those and iterate strictly within each concept's constraints.
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 5:30–5:43, 6:55–7:45

**NP-AS-04.** Never turn an ad off inside an ad set/campaign hitting KPI: Meta shows last-click but optimizes multi-touch, the "weak" ad is often a sequence assist or incrementality bet, and removing it resets learning at ad-set/campaign level. Below KPI, intervene freely — turning ads off is legitimate but usually low-leverage.
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 9:11–9:18, 11:38–12:13, 12:54–13:11

**NP-AS-05.** Breakdown views (placement/age/region) are blended last-click data with no incrementality: stories at 4x vs feed at 3x while feed holds 80% of spend is usually CORRECT — Meta already tested incremental spend on stories and got ~zero return. Don't segment off breakdowns unless you're a veteran buyer.
— source: "The Only Video You Need On Meta Ads In 2026" @ 6:56–9:06

**NP-AS-06.** Consolidation beats segmentation because conversion data is siloed at the campaign level (true on every ad platform). Add a campaign only for a commercial reason: a different funnel/persona (different ads AND landing pages), or a segment with better economics earning its own, more aggressive CPA target.
— source: "The Only Video You Need On Meta Ads In 2026" @ 12:25–12:32, 16:24–16:30, 58:04–1:00:55

**NP-AS-07.** Retargeting is recommended in ~90% of cases (90-day visitors with objection-handling, or existing customers), but its primary job is hygiene: keeping existing customers OUT of cold campaigns so Meta doesn't inflate spend there. Even sub-$50K, exclude existing customers from testing and set 7-day-click attribution for data integrity.
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 2:16–2:47; "The Only Video You Need On Meta Ads In 2026" @ 45:30–45:50

**NP-AS-08.** At $250K+/mo, a second ad account on cost caps (same pixel, similar structure) "resets" your auction bid while retaining most learnings — arbitraging bid inflation that appears near niche saturation. Source labels the mechanism believed-but-unproven.
— source: "Best Meta Ad Account Structure for eCommerce in 2025" @ 15:22–16:48

## Decision rules

- At or above KPI → no ad-level surgery; launch new things NEXT to it. "Never touch something that's working."
- Below KPI → intervene; turning ads off allowed but usually beaten by new creative/angle/offer.
- New campaign only with a written commercial reason — never to "test audiences."
- Exclude converted conversions/customers from every cold campaign from day one; attribution 7-day click.
- Ignore breakdown-tab reallocations unless a MEDIUM+ pattern backs them.

## Teaching nuggets

- The dashboard is a receipt, not the engine: you see last-click; Meta optimizes the multi-touch chain.
- Breakdown effect: averages lie about increments — a 4x placement at $100/day may be 0x on the next $100.
- Generate concepts wide, consolidate hard: of thousands of combos, expect 2–4 to scale.
- No micro-conclusions ("blue beats red") from single tests; KPI at concept level only.
