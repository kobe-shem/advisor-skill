# Ronny Mitchell — doctrine card

## Use this card for

Building a founder's personal brand as an authority asset: differentiation, audience strategy,
idea research, scripting, formats, creative direction, and media-team design. Use it when the risk
is becoming an interchangeable content creator who wins views but owns no distinct association.

Do not use this card to invent the user's beliefs, stories, proof, voice, offer, or ICP. Those come
from the user's own evidence and their live content and offer state. Do not use content as a
substitute for a cash-producing acquisition channel. Ronny owns the authority-and-media layer;
Hormozi owns current lead economics, offer, and cash constraints.

## Core doctrine — build a brand, not a content commodity

- **The job is not “make content”; it is solve a real problem for a chosen person.** Views without
  useful supply may grow an account, but they do not reliably create authority, trust, or customers.
  Start from the problem and the person, then use content as distribution for the solution.
  `[R01 00:08–00:11] [R12 00:00–00:05]`
- **A personal brand is a deliberately selected set of associations intended to influence a
  specific audience.** Decide what people should associate with the founder, then reinforce those
  associations through repeated proof, language, stories, and behavior. Influence is the outcome;
  reach is an input. `[R05 00:15–00:20, Ronny]`
- **Position as a leader people can learn from or emulate.** Competence, credibility, and connection
  make expertise believable; the resulting trust can create clients, partnerships, resilience, and
  pricing power. Those commercial outcomes are Ronny's self-reported operating claims, not
  independently verified results. `[R07 00:00–00:10]`
- **Authority has to come from the work.** A creator with no real-world experience can only recycle
  other people's conclusions. Earn differentiated “supply” through practice, decisions, results,
  mistakes, and exposure to edge cases, then turn tacit expertise into named frameworks and
  observable steps. `[R03 00:05–00:10] [R08 01:19–01:22, Ronny] [R10 00:00–00:05]`
- **Anti-commoditization is an evidence stack:** distinctive beliefs + lived stories + proof + a
  recognizable way of explaining the work. The strongest claims are things the founder can say
  because they have done what others have not. Packaging can borrow taste; the authority cannot be
  borrowed. `[R02 00:00–00:11] [R10 00:00–00:08] [R12 00:10–00:14]`
- **Expert versus reporter is a useful boundary, not unqualified Ronny doctrine.** In the Sam
  interview, Sam introduces the frame: teach from proven experience when qualified; otherwise
  document the experiment honestly. Ronny participates in the discussion, so the router labels it
  `joint / Sam-origin`. `[R08 01:10–01:14]`

## The 3D system

Ronny's system evolved in name while retaining the same functional middle layer:

1. **Demand:** choose a problem, question, or topic with evidence that the intended audience cares.
   Signals include outlier performance relative to account size, comments, FAQs, communities,
   Reddit, search, sales calls, applications, and repeated customer language. Demand validates the
   question, not your answer. `[R01 00:00–00:11] [R05 00:50–00:55, Ronny] [R12 00:00–00:05]`
2. **Delivery / Distribution:** package and communicate the answer so it works without hidden
   context. Use literal, observable hooks; simplify without hollowing out the idea; define terms;
   demonstrate where possible; and choose a format the founder can produce well and the audience can
   consume easily. Earlier videos call this **Distribution**; the July 2026 synthesis calls it
   **Delivery**. Treat these as a terminology change, not two separate frameworks.
   `[R12 00:05–00:10, Distribution] [R01 00:10–00:15, Delivery]`
3. **Differentiation:** own a defensible hill. Bring the founder's beliefs, experience, proof,
   stories, personality, and teaching devices to an in-demand topic so the audience has a reason to
   remember and choose this person rather than the next creator saying the same thing.
   `[R01 00:10–00:17] [R02 00:00–00:11] [R12 00:10–00:14]`

**Adaptation rule:** separate concept from packaging. It is legitimate to borrow an already-proven
demand signal and study an effective delivery device, then recombine them with the founder's own
answer. Copying both the premise and expression is imitation, not strategy. `[R01 00:00–00:05]`

## Audience architecture and success metrics

- **Broad and niche content do different jobs.** Narrow buyer-problem content can create customers
  now; broader adjacent content can educate future buyers and enlarge the pool that will care later.
  Do not grade both tracks by the same metric. `[R05 00:58–01:03, primarily Ronny/joint] [R10
  00:05–00:09]`
- **A creator has audiences within the audience.** Speak only to people the business is actually
  qualified to help, but vary the level of context: unaware/beginner explanations for reach,
  problem-aware education for trust, and expert material for qualified buyers and peers.
  `[R10 00:05–00:09] [R12 00:05–00:10]`
- **Useful repetition is not automatically redundancy.** Most potential audience members have not
  seen the founder's strongest idea, so a proven principle can be revisited with fresh evidence,
  stories, or packaging instead of being discarded after one post. `[R09 00:00] [R10 00:00–00:05]`
- **Track reach and customer outcomes separately.** Reach content is judged by qualified discovery,
  retention, follows, and association lift; buyer content by conversations, applications, pipeline,
  customer quality, and sales. A broad post is not a failure because it closes no immediate deal,
  and a niche post is not a failure because it never reaches millions. `[R05 00:58–01:03,
  primarily Ronny/joint]`
- **The mix is phase-dependent, not a fixed ratio.** A founder who needs cash weights buyer content
  and direct acquisition more heavily. Once the cash engine works, broad education can compound the
  future-buyer pool. `[R05 00:58–01:03] [R08 00:00–00:10, joint discussion]`

### ICP boundary

Ronny often teaches beginner-friendly breadth. That cannot override the ICP the user has chosen.
Inside a chosen ICP, “broad” means a larger share of that audience around their real problems, not
generic mass-audience self-improvement. Every public thesis, judgment, story, and proof point must
still trace to the user's own evidence; external demand research supplies the question and the
packaging wrapper only.

## Research and ideation

- **Require proof of demand before production.** Look for topics that outperform the creator's usual
  baseline, repeated questions, strong comment language, recurring sales objections, and phrases the
  customer already uses. A viral topic in the wrong audience is not valid demand. `[R01 00:00–00:11]
  [R05 00:50–00:55, Ronny] [R12 00:00–00:05]`
- **Analyze concept and packaging independently.** Ask first, “What underlying problem made this
  work?” and second, “What hook, sequence, visual, or format made it consumable?” Reuse the learning,
  not the artifact. `[R01 00:00–00:05] [R10 00:00–00:05]`
- **Translate expert abstractions into customer-observable language.** Replace undefined nouns and
  internal jargon with the behaviors, moments, symptoms, and decisions the audience recognizes.
  `[R01 00:10–00:15] [R03 00:15–00:20] [R12 00:05–00:10]`
- **Tasteful adaptation preserves the founder's authority.** Study structures, demand, and visual
  devices, but supply an original belief, example, story, proof, or mechanism. `[R01 00:00–00:05]
  [R02 00:00–00:11] [R10 00:00–00:05]`

## The seven scripting laws

Ronny presents these as a full-script discipline. Where a founder films to a lighter interface —
exact hook + exact payoff + riff bullets — apply the laws to the speaking card and keep that
interface. The laws govern logic; they do not require a teleprompter.

1. **Vein theory:** one clean narrative vein; remove facts, caveats, and tangents that obstruct it.
2. **Demand anchor:** open on a problem or desire with observable audience demand.
3. **Supply:** earn the promise with a real answer, mechanism, framework, example, or proof.
4. **Logic chain:** each line should make the next line necessary; the argument should move like a
   sequence, not a pile of soundbites.
5. **Defined terms:** explain or replace any word that requires expert context.
6. **Operationalized action:** convert advice into behavior someone can perform and verify.
7. **Polishing:** read aloud, cut drag, recheck the logic later through a stranger's eyes, and keep
   the payoff intact.

Source: `[R03 00:00–00:23]`; the logic-chain and operationalized-takeaway language also recurs in
`[R05 00:25–00:40, joint]`.

## The four content formats

Choose at the intersection of (a) easy and sustainable for the founder to create, (b) suited to the
founder's strengths, and (c) easy for the audience to consume. `[R01 00:10–00:15] [R11 00:00–00:21]`

1. **Podcast / conversation:** use an informed interviewer and real dialogue to elicit natural ideas
   and authoritative soundbites. The interviewer manages prompts and creator energy; the set is
   optional. `[R11 00:00–00:07]`
2. **Props:** show rather than merely tell. The object must make the teaching clearer, and changing
   props can create visual progression; irrelevant spectacle weakens the explanation.
   `[R11 00:05–00:12]`
3. **Demonstrations:** perform the real action or show the work in context. The founder/activity is
   the visual proof, and the lesson should remain legible without background knowledge.
   `[R11 00:10–00:17]`
4. **Direct-to-camera authority:** a higher-trust, higher-relationship format that relies on real
   expertise, personality, or presence. Use polished camera work when it adds authority or a low-
   friction phone setup when repetitions matter more. `[R11 00:15–00:21]`

Formats are containers, not strategy. Each piece still needs demand, a clear open loop/problem,
coherent story or utility, and a closed payoff. `[R06 00:05–00:15] [R11 00:00–00:21]`

## Creative operations and media-team scaling

- **A creative director owns the account outcome.** This is the accountable owner, not a narrow
  editor. They diagnose the current bottleneck, put attention there, coordinate people/process, and
  accept responsibility for performance. `[R04 00:00–00:05]`
- **The role bundles six capabilities:** narrative editing; camera/visual craft; creative strategy;
  conversational threading and on-camera direction; turning expertise into teachable process; and
  managing creative work through other people. A gap in one can bottleneck the whole account.
  `[R04 00:00–00:15]`
- **Run tight feedback loops.** Study winners and failures, change one meaningful variable at a time,
  keep what improves the desired metric, and turn the learning into repeatable process. Volume is
  useful when it accelerates reps and evidence, not when it merely fills a calendar.
  `[R04 00:10–00:14] [R08 01:21–01:25, Ronny]`
- **Scale around a dedicated number two, then specialize.** The founder remains the source of
  authority. A trusted operator learns the founder deeply, owns the creative system, and later adds
  specialists only where workload or skill gaps justify them. `[R08 01:19–01:25, Ronny]`
- **Do not outsource the founder's primary craft.** A media team can extract, package, and distribute
  expertise; it cannot manufacture the experience, decisions, or proof that make the founder worth
  following. `[R08 01:10–01:22, mixed: Sam-origin + Ronny] [R10 00:00–00:05]`

## Time-sensitive 2026 operating hypotheses — not permanent doctrine

Treat these as dated tactics to retest by platform and account, never as timeless rules:

- Ronny/Sam rank hashtags and posting time as low-leverage relative to topic, communication, and
  substance. `[R05 00:00–00:15, joint]`
- Short-form thumbnails and fancy editing are often lower priority than the idea and delivery;
  production value can be increased after substance works. `[R05 00:00–00:15, joint] [R10
  00:05–00:09]`
- High posting volume is most defensible as a way to gain repetitions, collect feedback, and find a
  working format. It is not a substitute for quality supply. `[R05 00:10–00:15, joint] [R06
  00:15–00:18]`
- Raw/FaceTime, ranking, trial-reel, and other favored formats/features are snapshots of 2026
  conditions and may decay. `[R05 00:10–00:15, joint] [R11 00:15–00:21]`
- **Repurposing attribution:** Ronny introduces the segment, but Sam makes the substantive claim that

## Decision rules

- If a content idea has no evidence of demand, research the audience problem before scripting.
  `[R01 00:00–00:11]`
- If demand is proven but retention is weak, diagnose delivery/distribution before changing the
  thesis. `[R01 00:10–00:15] [R12 00:05–00:10]`
- If reach is strong but the account is forgettable, add differentiated belief, experience, proof,
  story, or a recognizable teaching device. `[R02 00:00–00:11]`
- If views rise but qualified conversations do not, separate the reach and buyer tracks and check
  whether the content solves a buyer problem. `[R05 00:58–01:03, primarily Ronny/joint]`
- If the founder cannot substantiate a claim, report the experiment or question instead of posing as
  an expert. `[R08 01:10–01:14, joint/Sam-origin]`
- If a script feels generic, locate the missing supply: original judgment, mechanism, evidence,
  example, or operational step. `[R03 00:05–00:20]`
- If production is hard to sustain, choose a different format before hiring a large team.
  `[R01 00:10–00:15] [R11 00:00–00:21]`
- If creative work lacks one accountable owner, install a dedicated number two before adding a
  collection of disconnected specialists. `[R04 00:00–00:15] [R08 01:19–01:25, Ronny]`
- If content competes with near-term lead generation for scarce time, keep the cash-producing channel
  funded and build content in parallel as a long-horizon authority asset. `[R07 00:00–00:12]
  [R08 00:00–00:10, joint]`

## Application boundaries and conflicts

- **Breadth:** Ronny optimizes for discoverability and a future-buyer pool. The user's chosen ICP
  wins; broaden only inside that market and its adjacent problems.
- **Scripting:** Ronny's full-script craft informs structure. Where the user has an established
  filming interface, that interface remains the production standard.
- **Time horizon:** Ronny treats content as compounding authority. Hormozi's Core Four and current
  lead economics still fund the business now. Content complements outbound, referrals, partnerships,
  and paid channels; it does not automatically replace them.
- **Source law:** external Ronny doctrine may recommend topic selection, packaging, format, and team
  design. It may not originate the user's public thesis, first-person story, proof, or voice, and it
  must not automatically rewrite the user's own content system.
